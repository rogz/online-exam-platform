  • Switching tabs or windows will VOID your exam. This includes opening another tab, minimizing the browser, using Alt+Tab / Cmd+Tab, or clicking another application.
  • Multiple monitors are prohibited. Disconnect any second monitor, TV or projector before starting. Connecting one during the exam will void it.

  • The timer starts when you click Start exam. When time runs out, your answers are submitted automatically.
  • Copy, paste and right-click are disabled during the exam.
  • If your exam is voided, inform your instructor. Only your instructor can let you take it again.
