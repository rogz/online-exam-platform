The student dashboard

How it works

Getting a class started takes a few minutes.

For students

Free, no setup needed

  1. Create a free accountYour name, email and a password.
  2. Join your classType the 6-character class code your teacher gives you.
  3. Take your examsThey appear on your dashboard automatically, and open on time.

For teachers

Start with an access code or credits

  1. Create a classEach class gets its own join code to share with students.
  2. Build your question bankMultiple choice, true/false, fill in the blank and essays, with pictures and topics. Import from a spreadsheet.
  3. Schedule an examSet when it opens and closes, the time limit, and the security you want.
  4. See results instantlyScores are calculated on submit. Grade essays, allow retakes, download to Excel.

The exam screen

A secure exam screen

Students are told the rules before they start. Every event is logged for the teacher.

  • Leaving the exam voids itSwitching tabs or apps, Alt+Tab, or a second monitor ends the attempt.
  • Optional camera checkFace visible at the start, or throughout: warnings (you choose how many), then void. No video is recorded.
  • Fair timingA countdown on the server's clock, auto-submit at zero, and warnings before time runs out.
  • Bad internet isn't a failureStudents can reconnect within a minute and continue where they left off.
