Online exams for your classes

Teachers build timed quizzes and exams from their own question bank. Students see every exam from all their classes in one place, and take them in a secure exam screen.

One list for studentsUpcoming, open and completed exams from every class, in one side panel.
Automatic gradingMultiple choice, true/false and fill-in-the-blank are scored instantly. Essays are graded by the teacher.
Exam integrityTab-switch and second-monitor detection, optional camera check, time limits and exam passwords.
Question bankReuse questions with topics and pictures, and give each student a random set.
